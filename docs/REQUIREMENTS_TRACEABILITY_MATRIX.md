# SPECTRA Requirements Traceability Matrix

## Purpose

This matrix connects final-release engineering requirements to implemented
software, verification evidence, current status, and known limitations.
SPECTRA is a receive-only RF observation, survey, and deterministic
decision-support system. It does not identify transmitters or modulation,
provide calibrated dBm, or establish detector accuracy without controlled
ground truth.

## Status Definitions

- **Complete — validated:** implemented and supported by automated or recorded
  validation evidence.
- **Complete — operationally verified:** implemented and exercised with the
  RTL-SDR, but not calibrated against controlled RF ground truth.
- **Complete — characterized limitation:** implemented and tested, with a
  preserved limitation or failed acceptance criterion.

## Coverage Summary

All fifteen release requirements have an implementation location and supporting
evidence. Requirements involving deterministic software behavior are covered by
automated tests and synthetic validation. Hardware-facing requirements are
operationally verified with the RTL-SDR but remain subject to receiver,
environment, and calibration limitations. Negative synthetic results are
retained explicitly and do not invalidate unrelated functional requirements.
