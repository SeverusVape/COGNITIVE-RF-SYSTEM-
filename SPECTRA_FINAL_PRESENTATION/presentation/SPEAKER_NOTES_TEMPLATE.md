## Global Presenter Language

Prefer:

- “relative power”;
- “spectral-bin occupancy”;
- “peak candidate”;
- “context descriptor”;
- “deterministic SMART scoring”;
- “score separation”; and
- “recorded agreement and runtime comparison.”

Avoid:

- calibrated dBm;
- signal identification;
- statistical confidence;
- AI or machine learning;
- autonomous spectrum access; and
- absolute accuracy without ground truth.
